# What works on World in a Shot

Updated 2026-11-28, based on 11 videos.

- Prioritize high YouTube demand topics: Stories with existing demand on YouTube tend to generate significantly higher 2-day view counts.
- Schedule uploads for the 18:00 UTC slot: The 18:00 UTC publishing window generated the highest average view counts overall.
- Focus on Tech news: Tech-related stories (e.g., Apple, Tesla) show strong viewership and high audience retention rates.
- Include specific brands, numbers, or prices in titles: Precise details like '$1,999', 'Apple', or '89-Year-Old' perform better than generic titles.
- Deliver the core premise in the first sentence: Direct, clear hooks stating the key fact immediately retain viewers effectively.
